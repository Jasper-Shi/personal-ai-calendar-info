Personal AI Calendar Sync

This integration is for the account owner's personal use. Access requires the owner's Google authorization and a privately operated Personal AI backend. No public sign-up service is offered.

Calendar interpretation

The owner chooses to treat calendar entries as personal activities, including planned future events. Scheduled event times and durations are not independent attendance measurements. Cancellations, declined invitations and missing source entries retain their distinct status.

Availability and control

Synchronization depends on the owner's backend, Google API availability and a valid authorization. The approximate 15-minute interval is not an availability guarantee. The owner can stop synchronization or revoke access at any time. The integration does not modify Google Calendar events.

Privacy and changes

The privacy policy describes data handling. Changes that require new permissions must be separately authorized by the owner. This integration is independent of Google and is not endorsed by Google.
