Privacy policy · Effective October 17, 2026

How calendar data is handled

Personal AI Calendar Sync is a personal-use integration operated by the Google account owner. This policy describes the integration's access, storage, use and sharing of Google user data.

Data accessed

After the owner grants permission, the integration reads primary-calendar event identifiers, titles, descriptions, locations, dates, time zones, recurrence details, status and the owner's invitation response. It uses the Google account's stable identifier to prevent accidental account mixing. It does not request Google Calendar write access.

Purpose and use

Events become activity records for the owner's personal history, planning and owner-directed analysis. Past calendar events are treated as completed under the owner's explicit policy, while future events remain upcoming. The integration does not sell data, use it for advertising or train machine learning models.

Storage and protection

Activity records and synchronization state are stored in the owner's private Personal AI backend. Google OAuth credentials are encrypted there using a private key ring. Network requests to Google use HTTPS. Credentials are not exposed through the AI query interface, published on this site or committed to the source repository.

Sharing and connected tools

The owner may enable connected AI services to read activity records for personal queries and reports. Those services process the records under their own terms and the owner's account settings. The integration does not grant third parties independent access, and the owner controls the Activities permission switch. Authorized backups may retain copies of records.

Retention and deletion

Imported history remains in the private database until the owner deletes it. The owner can disable synchronization, delete activity records through the Personal AI controls, or revoke Personal AI Calendar Sync in the Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revocation stops future authorized reads; it does not automatically delete existing records or backups. Deleting a local activity does not delete the Google event, and the integration remembers the deletion so later synchronization does not recreate that activity.

Google API Limited Use

Personal AI Calendar Sync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This website and contact

This static website has no forms, analytics scripts or application cookies and accepts no calendar data. Its hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process standard web request logs under its own privacy statement. For questions, contact the application operator using the support address shown on Google's consent screen.
